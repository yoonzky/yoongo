# Third-party notices

Yoongo's own code and its generated problems (`data/problems.js`) are under AGPL-3.0, see `LICENSE`. The classic collections in `data/classics.js` are not. They are listed here with their sources.

## Xuanxuan Qijing

- The book: compiled by Yan Defu and Yan Tianzhang, China, 1349. Public domain.
- The SGF: problems put into SGF by Jean-Pierre Vesinet, distributed by Ulrich Görtz at https://u-go.net/classic/ (`xxqj.sgf.gz`). No license is stated for the file.
- In Yoongo: 333 of 347 problems. The rest were left out because a solution line breaks the rules or is longer than 41 moves.

## Guanzi Pu

- The book: compiled by Guo Bailing, China, 17th century. Public domain.
- The SGF: files from the Flygo site (http://www.flygo.net/), merged without the Chinese comments and distributed at https://u-go.net/classic/ (`gzp1`, `gzp2`, `gzp3`). u-go.net thanks Flygo for permission to use and publish the files. No license is stated for the files.
- In Yoongo: 1467 of 1477 problems, filtered the same way.

## Changes made

Colours are swapped where the book has White to play, so that Black always starts. Board positions and solution lines are otherwise as in the files. Every move of every line was checked against the rules.

If you hold rights to these files and want them credited differently or removed, open an issue at https://github.com/yoonzky/yoongo/issues.
